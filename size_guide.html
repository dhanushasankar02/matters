Bedroom Design Rules

Walking Clearance & Furniture Placement

Follow these 4 spatial rules to prevent cramped bedroom traffic flow.

24"

Perimeter Walking Rule

Leave at least 24 inches of open floor space on both sides and foot of the bed.

36"

Door Swing Clearance

Ensure bedroom doors and closet doors open 90 degrees without hitting the mattress edge.

18"

Nightstand Placement

Position nightstands 2-4 inches away from mattress side to prevent sheet snagging.

25"

Ideal Sitting Height

Bed frame + mattress total height should equal 25 inches from floor for easy sitting.
