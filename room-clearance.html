Natural Airflow & Window Safety

Headboard Window & HVAC Vent Rules

Key architectural rules for positioning beds near windows and heat vents.

01

Window Sill Gap Rule

Ensure the top edge of your headboard rests either 4 inches below the window sill or covers the window trim symmetrically to allow drape clearance.

02

HVAC Floor Register Airflow

Keep solid platform bases 6 inches away from AC floor vents. Air obstruction reduces HVAC cooling efficiency by up to 35%.

03

Baseboard Radiator Safety

Maintain a minimum 12-inch gap between electric baseboard heaters and mattress side borders to protect latex cores.

04

Wall Electrical Outlet Access

Leave 3 inches behind nightstands to prevent bent cords when plugging in bedside lamps and phone chargers.
